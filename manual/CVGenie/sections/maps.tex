\subsection{Cartridge and revision compatibility}
The added maps target the original North American NES cartridges; Tetris
means Nintendo's version. Famicom Disk System releases, regional variants,
ROM hacks, and other revisions may use different addresses. The older Mario
and Zelda maps do not identify a precise ROM revision. Genie neither detects
nor verifies the ROM. These maps were checked against RAM documentation;
that does not establish gameplay testing of every control or revision.

RackNES currently supports cartridge mappers 0--3, 4 (MMC3 / TxROM),
7 (AxROM / AOROM), and 9 (MMC2 / PxROM). The RackNES manual describes the
accepted board and NES 2.0 layouts. MMC5 (5) and FME-7 / Sunsoft 5B (69) are
not implemented, and cartridge expansion sound is unavailable.
Mapper support does not guarantee that every game or board variant works;
adding a memory map cannot supply missing emulation behavior. An installed
VCV Library build may also predate these mapper additions. The game list is a
curated selection, not a percentage of all NES games or a guarantee that any
ROM bearing one of these names works.

\subsection{Useful starting points}
Try Mega Man's health or weapon energy, Contra's player positions and weapons,
or Metroid's per-channel music frame timers. A held timer can stop progression
until its cable is disconnected. Contra's weapon controls select base weapons
and clear rapid fire. Metroid's Missile Mode does not grant missiles.

Start during active play with one row connected and a RackNES SAVE snapshot
ready. Position writes can move a character into walls or place a Tetris piece
across occupied cells; range limits cannot enforce the rules of the current
scene. Games may overwrite a value or use its address differently in menus.
Disconnect inputs before restoring a snapshot. When reporting an incorrect
mapping, include the ROM region/revision, selected element, voltage, and scene.
