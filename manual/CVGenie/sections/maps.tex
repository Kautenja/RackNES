\manualchapter{Built-in game maps}{sec:maps}
These tables list every selectable entry in menu order. Addresses are
hexadecimal; First and Second are decimal byte values. For continuous
entries (\textbf{C}), First corresponds to 0 V and Second to 10 V. For
toggles (\textbf{T}), rising triggers alternate between these two values.

The maps contain 53 Mario entries and 55 Zelda entries. They identify no
specific ROM region or revision, and Genie does not check the ROM's identity.
A supported mapper in RackNES does not establish that a memory map matches
that ROM. The names and addresses below are the ones shipped in the code.
